# P2 Review — Instagram Growth Strategist

**Series:** Petal's Adventures (5 × 30s Reels, 9:16)
**Reviewer lens:** hook strength (0–2s), first-frame / cover thumbnail, retention curve, loopability, mute legibility, shareability.
**Sources read:** `story/series_bible.md`, `story/petal_visual_traits.md`, `story/ep01_script.md` … `ep05_script.md`
**Threshold:** 8 on every criterion. 9–10 is reserved for exceptional work.

---

## Verdict

**No episode passes yet.** The creative foundation is strong: the character is specific, the gag library is good, the arc has a real payoff, and Dubai is used well. The failures are almost all **packaging and legibility**, which is where a Reel succeeds or dies:

1. **Frame 0 often lacks Petal, or anything readable.** EP5 opens on black, EP4 on sand with no character, EP3 on the cat, and EP1 on a half-closed eye. The first frame is also the grid cover and the Explore tile.
2. **The gift-counter logic is broken on mute.** The arc is titled "15 Smells" but has 5 tags. EP5's box holds 4 items, the tag reads 5/5, and the caption says "Smell #15". A cold viewer can't decode it, and an invested viewer will post "wait, how is that 15?" comments. That is negative engagement.
3. **There are mid-video dead zones.** Several 6-second shots carry no caption and only slow action (EP1 2–14s, EP3 2–8s). On a 30s Reel, the 3–8s window is where most of the drop-off happens.
4. **Gift pocketing is too small, too fast or off-screen.** That makes the `🎁 X/5` tag meaningless.
5. **Two of five loops don't actually loop.** In EP3 and EP4 the end state does not match frame 1.

Fix those five things and EP2, EP3 and EP4 clear 8 across the board. EP1 and EP5 also need logic fixes.

## Series-wide required fixes (apply before per-episode notes)

**S1. Repair the gift count (blocking).**
- Retitle the arc to **"5 Smells for 15 Years"**, or drop the number from the title.
- The tags become `🎁 1/5` to `🎁 4/5` for EP1–4. **Jamal is gift 5/5.**
- In EP5 (19–25s), change "Smell #15: home." to **"Smell #5: home."** Better still, use **"The last smell: home."**
- The EP5 tag at 25–30s stays "🎁 5/5".
- Now the box shows 4 items, Jamal makes 5, and the tag says 5/5. It adds up on mute.

**S2. Frame-0 rule.** Every episode's first frame must contain:
- Petal's face with the forehead mark visible (the series' brand identifier), OR
- a clear stake, with Petal in frame.

Nothing may be black, half-closed or empty. The opening caption is ≤5 words and is readable in 1s.

**S3. Gift-pocket rule.** Each collection beat needs:
- ≥1.0s of screen time;
- the item centred in the safe area;
- a mini push-in;
- a sparkle sting;
- a labelled tag: `🎁 1/5 · Marina breeze`, `🎁 2/5 · cardamom`, and so on.

Nothing happens off-screen.

**S4. Series premise card for cold viewers.**
- In EP1 (25–30s), add one line under the tag: **"Secret mission: 5 smells for her 15th 🤫"**.
- Pin a comment on every episode: "Episode X of 5 · Petal turns 15 on [date] 🎁".
- Without this, `🎁 1/5` is noise.

**S5. Kill dead zones.** No shot runs longer than 4s without either a caption change or a visible gag beat. The flagged instances are listed per episode.

**S6. Surface the "send-to" line.** The Send-to lines are currently only in the script notes. Use them as:
- the first line of the IG caption, or
- an on-screen sticker in the last 2s.

That is where shares are won.

**S7. Cover frames.** Export a dedicated cover for each episode, with Petal's face and a ≤4-word title in the centre 1:1 crop. The grid crops 9:16 to 3:4, and the profile grid then crops to 1:1. Keep the title inside the centre square.

- **19.0–25.0 (logic):**
  - Biscuit **faceplants into the planter 1 metre before** the chalk finish line, and Petal sleep-rolls across.
  - Add a finish-line insert with Mochi's 🏆 *on Petal*.
  - The caption "Work smarter. Not harder." stays.
- **25.0–30.0:**
  - Give the lump beat (nap copy) 2.5s on its own.
  - Then show the palm-leaf pocket for 1.0s, centred, with sparkle and the label "🎁 1/5 · Marina breeze".
  - Add the premise line from S4.
  - Make the ECU eye snap the final 0.5s.
- **Cultural:** put Petal and Biscuit on visible slack leashes held by off-screen hands, or move the race to a beach/park setting that is clearly pet-friendly. This neutralises the "rules" comment thread.
- **Loop:** keep as is.

## Posting playbook (quick)

- **Cadence:** every 2 days, or Mon/Wed/Fri/Sun/Tue, with EP5 on a fixed "Gotcha Day" date announced in EP1's pinned comment.
- **Time:** post at 19:00–21:00 Gulf Standard Time (GST).
- **Covers:** use a custom cover for each episode, with Petal's face and the forehead mark in the centre 1:1. Never use frame 0 of EP5.
- **Series consistency:** add a `🎁 X/5` badge in the same corner of every cover so the grid reads as a set.
- **Trial Reels:** A/B two hook-text variants per episode via Trial Reels. Keep the one with a higher 3s hold rate.
- **Comment seeding:** pin a comment on each episode, then reply to early comments within the first 60 minutes.
