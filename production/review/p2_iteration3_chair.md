## 6. Advisories (not scored down)
- **A1:** EP2 shows "lil Petal, 2011" in the souk. If B4c is skipped, expect "wasn't she found in the desert?" comments.
- **A2:** EP2's water bowl now exists only for the paw prints. That's fine, but keep the bowl clearly a *dog* bowl outside a stall, not a vendor's container.
- **A3:** EP3 shows Sultana soaked on the bed, then Petal nudges the wet bed into the sun. The reading "giving her the warm spot to dry" only works if the light clearly moves onto the bed. Light it that way.
- **A4:** EP4 has no cool-season cue and no ghaf trees in the flashback. Adding both would lift cultural fit to 9. This is the cheapest upgrade available.
- **A5:** EP5's flash cuts at 12–18 are fast for cold viewers. Use 4 × 1.2s cuts tinted in each giver's colour, and label the box lid "5 Smells for 15 Years". That would lift Mute to 9.
- **A6:** "Grandma" appears in three of five hooks (EP1, EP2 and EP4). Do not add a fourth.
- **A7:** Render the Arabic tag "جمل" as connected, right-to-left glyphs. Check it in the render, because 3D text tools often break Arabic shaping.
- **A8:** No adhan or crescents appear in EP2 or EP5. Keep them out of the sound and art checklists.
