# P2 Review: Gen-Z Audience Tester (humour · pacing · cringe)

**Reviewer:** Gen-Z Audience Tester, engagement review panel
**Scope:** `series_bible.md`, `ep01_script.md` to `ep05_script.md`
**Threshold:** 8 on every criterion. **Result: no episode passes as written.** Ep 4 comes closest (one 7). The core idea is good: a senior rescue dog, the "old isn't slow, it's knowing" angle, and a 5-part gift countdown. A Gen-Z viewer would follow and share this. The problems are fixable, and most of them are execution:

1. **Overstuffed 25–30s buttons.** Every episode puts the lump beat, the gift hand-off, a callback gag and the loop match into 5 seconds. Something gets lost every time, and it's usually the 🎁 hand-off, which the finale depends on.
2. **Dead air in the 2–8s window.** That's where Reels lose the most viewers (Ep 3 especially).
3. **A few captions that sound like a brand or a hustle-culture account** ("Work smarter. Not harder.") or that are too long to read in their slot.
4. **The arc's numbers don't add up.** The arc is called "15 Smells for 15 Years". The show collects 4 smells plus Jamal, the counter reads 🎁 5/5, and the finale caption says "Smell #15". Comments will fill up with "wait, it's 5??", and that's confusion, not engagement.
5. **The finale twist isn't set up.** "She knew. Every time." only pays off if every episode shows Petal *noticing* the gift. Right now only Ep 1 (eye snap) and Ep 2 (nose push-in) come close.

## EP 1: The Senior Zoomie (avg 7.9)

**What works:** The eye-snap hook-and-loop is the best loop in the series. The zoomie-then-flop gag is the exact kind of thing that gets re-watched to catch the frame where she drops. Biscuit copying her nap is adorable.

### Fixes for scores below 8
- **Emotional payoff 7.** **25.0–30.0:** the nap copy is fighting with the flower-on-head gag, the leaf tuck and the ECU cut. *Change:* lose the flower on his head; the planter faceplant at 19–25 already covers it. Hold the nap-copy two-shot for a full 2.5s (25.0–27.5). Move the palm-leaf tuck to an insert at **27.5–28.5**, then show Petal's ECU eye **flicking to the leaf before it snaps open** (28.5–30.0). That also plants the S2 "she noticed" receipt the bible asks for ("Petal's eye 'notices' Biscuit pocket it"), which the script currently skips.
- **Mute clarity 7.** **8.0–14.0:** the finish line is never shown, so at 19–25 the sleep-roll "across the line" reads as random. *Change:* at 8.0–9.0, open on a quick establishing tilt down the promenade to a **chalk FINISH line and a tiny Mochi flag 3 dog-lengths away**. Plant it so the flop at 14–19 lands *just short* of the line. Then the sleep-roll win is a payoff the viewer can see coming.
- **Cultural fit 7.** The "Work smarter. Not harder." caption at 19–25 is LinkedIn/hustle-bro energy and Gen-Z will clock it instantly. Rewrite below. Also, the Marina reads as "any beachfront": add a **dhow or abra silhouette on the water** at 2.0–8.0 for a cheap local signal (no landmarks or brands needed).

- **Emotional payoff 7.** **19.0–25.0:** Jamal being "given" to her has no setup, because she carried him in Eps 1 and 3. Why does she need him given back? *Change:* at **8.0–13.0**, Petal enters **without Jamal** and does a tiny glance at her empty paws/the yellow bed (0.5s). Then the hand returning Jamal is a payoff, not a non-sequitur. Optional: the hand had been hiding him so he'd smell like the human, which is exactly why "home" makes sense.

This is the strongest gag available in the series and it's currently missing.
