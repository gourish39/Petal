**Threshold:** 8 on every criterion. **Result: no episode clears it yet.** EP1 comes closest. The series has a strong engine (senior-dog flex plus a found-family heist). What sinks it is three things: (1) the secret-gift arc can't be read on mute, (2) two of the hooks are generic, with no Petal and no stakes, and (3) the finale is coded, by accident, like a rainbow-bridge video.

## 2. Series-level problems (fix these once, and several scores rise)

1. **On mute, the arc doesn't exist.** Nothing in EP1–4 tells a viewer that the friends are secretly planning a Gotcha Day. `🎁 1/5` with no context reads as a series counter or a sponsored-gift tag. Each gift tuck is also a tiny background action at 25–30s, which is when most viewers are already swiping. *Fix:* the first time the tag appears (EP1 25–30s), the caption reads **"Secret Gotcha Day gift 1/5 🤫"**. In EP2–4 it reads **"Secret gift 2/5: cardamom 🤫"** and so on, with the item named and the prop held in a 0.5s insert, centre frame.
2. **The gift count is broken.** The arc is called "15 Smells for 15 Years". Four smells are collected on screen, the 🎁 tags run to 5, and the finale caption says "Smell #15". Pet audiences *will* count in the comments, and the top comment becomes "wait, where are the other 10?" *Fix:* make the box hold **14 labelled scent pouches** (the gang's 4 hero pouches up front, plus 10 small tins labelled with years, 2011–2025). Jamal is then plainly #15. Or retitle the arc **"5 Gifts for 15 Years"** and caption the finale **"Gift 5/5: home"**. Pick one and keep it consistent across all tags.
3. **Jamal isn't a gift, because she already has him.** He's between her paws in EP1 and in her mouth in EP3. A hand "giving" her a toy she owns lands as nothing. *Fix:* set up a loss in EP4. At 20–25s Petal **puts Jamal down in the sand** to lead the others; Biscuit looks back at him; the haze swallows him. In EP5 the human's hand brings him back, washed, still with one ear. Now "home" is earned and it pays off a real fear.
4. **Rainbow-bridge coding (the biggest risk; see section 3).** Every lump beat that combines eyes closed + Noor crying + stillness/darkness reads as "this dog died" to anyone who has lost a senior dog. That's a large share of the audience you want.
5. **Leash and location realism (Dubai comments).** Dubai dog owners know the public-space dog rules inside out, and an off-leash dog on the Marina promenade or in the souk *will* start a comment-section debate about where dogs are allowed. That debate steals the thread from Petal. *Fix:* in public sets (EP1, EP2), show the **black leash clipped to the lime harness** trailing to frame edge (the human stays off-screen as a hand/leash), or open with a 0.5s "human hand unclips at the dog-friendly zone" beat. It costs nothing and protects the comments.
6. **Give the series one Arabic word per episode** for local reach and warmth: EP1 "Yalla!" (Mochi's GO flag), EP2 "Shukran" (Kebab's bow), EP3 "Khalas." (instead of "Fine."), EP4 "Yalla, habibti" (Biscuit to Petal, a caption bubble), EP5 "Mabrook Petal!" (banner). Arabic-English code-switching is how Dubai actually talks, and it makes the content clearly Dubai rather than generic.
