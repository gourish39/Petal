# Petal — Visual Traits (from reference photos + video)

Source: `refs/petal/01–07, 09` (8 photos) + `10_video.mov` (13.6s). The car selfie (`refs/private/08`) shows a real person, so it is **never** uploaded or used as a generation reference.

## Breed / build
- **Mixed breed.** Reads as a Desi / Indian-pariah × Labrador-type mix with spitz traits (tail). Medium-large, around 20–25 kg.
- **Body:** a deep chest and a **round barrel belly** that gives her a soft, heavy midsection, on **long, slim legs**. Her head is broad and domed. Her back is level and dips slightly with age.
- **Silhouette signature:** a round body on stick-like legs, plus the **curled plume tail**. Recognisable as a black shape.

## Coat
- Short-to-medium, dense, **predominantly white / warm cream**. The cream tone is warmer on her chest and neck.
- **Freckle ticking:** small **tan/ginger roan freckles** are scattered over her back, shoulders, hips, and down her legs (her "Dalmatian-in-sepia" look).
- **The Patch (key identifier):** one large, **dark taupe / grey-brown oval patch** on her flank just behind the shoulder, about palm-sized. A smaller grey spot sits at the base of her tail.
- **Tail:** a **thick, fluffy white plume curled up over her back** in a sickle/ring. It is the most expressive part of her body (see video: it wags in a full circle).

## Head & face
- A **white face and muzzle**, with **grey-taupe and tan "mask" shading** over both temples and around the eyes that blends into the ears. Tan freckles dot the muzzle and bridge of the nose.
- **Brow mark (key identifier):** a small **dark brown mole/scab-like spot on the forehead**, just above and between the eyes, slightly off-centre. Keep it in every close-up.
- **Nose:** large, dark liver-brown/charcoal, slightly matte with a hint of pink depigmentation at the edges.
- **Eyes:** dark brown with a **soft blue-grey cloudy haze** (senior lens clouding), and dark eye rims. These are canon "cloudy-wise eyes": gentle, knowing, a little droopy.
- **Ears:** medium, **soft semi-drop/rose ears** that fold forward and sideways. Tan-taupe with grey and freckles; the inner tips are a little darker.
- **Expression range seen:** a big open-mouth grin with a long pink tongue (her happy face in the video); a dignified "front sit" stare; a downcast side-glance (perfect for the side-eye); and a curious head tilt.

## Age signs (render them with love)
- An all-white, slightly frosted muzzle and brows, and cloudy eyes.
- Softness around the jowls and a slightly saggy belly.
- **Stiff hind legs.** She sits with her back legs splayed slightly outward (photo 07) and stands with a slight "bow" in her front legs.
- A slow, deliberate gait that becomes a happy trot when excited (video ending).
- *(Small lumps visible in photos are not featured.)*

## Costume / props
- **Signature harness:** a **lime/olive-green padded harness with grey trim and a red buckle**, often worn with a black leash. This is her "adventure outfit".
- Home textures: a pale beige tile floor, a **yellow velvet dog bed**, a grey plush bed, and white marble. These are useful for the home-base set.

## Motion notes (from video)
- She sits fast and eagerly when she expects a treat, with her front paws planted wide.
- Her head bobs with excitement, her tongue lolls, and her eyes track the camera.
- She **spins and trots away with her tail curled high**. That is the "senior zoomie" reference.
