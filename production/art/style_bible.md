# Style Bible: Petal's Adventures (Production Designer)

## Look
- **Medium:** a stylised 3D feature-animation look, like a premium family animated film. Characters are appealing, rounded and caricatured, never photoreal and never plastic.
- **Fur:** soft, groomed, readable clumps with subsurface glow at the ears and rim. Petal's coat is warm cream-white (#F4EBDD), never pure #FFFFFF, so the highlights don't clip.
- **Eyes:** large and expressive, with a clear catchlight. Petal's eyes are dark brown with a gentle blue-grey senior haze, and they must still read as warm.
- **Proportions:** a slightly enlarged head (about 1.2×). Each character keeps a signature silhouette (see the bible).
- **Lighting:** cinematic with a warm key, a cool fill and strong rim light. Use soft volumetric haze outdoors.

## Rules
- Use 9:16 vertical composition. Faces and text stay inside the centre-safe area: avoid the top 220px and the bottom 420px.
- Petal's identifiers appear in every shot where she's visible: the flank patch, the forehead mark, the curled plume tail and the lime harness.
- No brand logos, no readable real signage, no religious symbols or flags, and no recognisable humans. The human appears only as a neutral hand.
- Senior-dog dignity: show stiffness with charm, never frailty or illness.
